Energy Statistics in Ireland

Introduction

Household energy statistics help explain how energy is used in Irish homes. They also show why improving energy efficiency and increasing the use of renewable energy are important for Ireland.

Heating, hot water and electrical appliances are among the main sources of household energy consumption. Older homes may require more energy because of poor insulation or inefficient heating systems.

Household Energy Information

Main Areas of Household Energy Use
Area Energy Use Possible Improvement
Space Heating Energy used to heat rooms and maintain a comfortable indoor temperature. Better insulation, efficient heating controls and heat pumps.
Water Heating Energy used to provide hot water for washing, cleaning and other household activities. Efficient boilers, shorter showers and insulated hot-water tanks.
Electrical Appliances Electricity used by lighting, kitchen appliances, computers and entertainment equipment. Energy-efficient appliances and switching off unused devices.
Renewable Energy Energy produced from sources such as wind and solar power. Solar panels and electricity supplied from renewable sources.

Analysis

The information shows that household energy consumption can be reduced through improved insulation, efficient heating systems and responsible use of electrical appliances.

These improvements can help households reduce energy waste, lower their energy costs and support Ireland's progress towards Sustainable Development Goal 7.

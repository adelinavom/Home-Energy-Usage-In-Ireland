Estimate Your Electricity Costs

Enter your average monthly electricity consumption and electricity unit price to estimate your monthly and annual electricity costs.

This calculator provides an estimate only. Standing charges, discounts and other supplier fees are not included.

How the Calculation Works

The monthly electricity cost is calculated by multiplying the monthly electricity consumption by the electricity unit price.

The annual cost and annual consumption are estimated by multiplying the monthly results by 12.
